# 01 — Product foundation

## North star

IMOV will evolve into a digital ecosystem that helps people **discover digital products, connect with builders, and eventually create software with AI assistance**.

### Current positioning statement (proposed)

IMOV connects people looking for practical software solutions with digital products and development services, with a longer-term vision of AI-assisted creation.

### Brand line (proposed)

**Build what's next.**

Supporting text: **Discover digital products, connect with developers, and bring your next idea to life.**

Avoid positioning IMOV as an already-established platform with proven network effects.

## First target audiences

| Audience | Job to be done | Key concerns | Immediate action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyers: indie founders, developers, SMEs, students | Find a codebase/template or development support | Reliability, relevance, licensing, clarity | Explore product categories or service directions |
| Sellers: indie developers/freelancers | Showcase or eventually sell software and services | Reach, visibility, fair rules, payouts | Learn planned seller experience / express interest when a real channel exists |
| Potential partners/investors | Understand opportunity and product ambition | Market, team execution, differentiation, feasibility | Read vision and reach a verified contact channel |

**Launch priority:** buyers' discovery experience. Seller workflows and real commerce follow when there is genuine inventory and operational capability.

## Three pillars and readiness

1. **Digital Products — planned core:** discovery and eventually purchase of software templates, source code, starter kits, components, and related assets with clear licensing.
2. **Development Services — planned core:** help users find builders or services suited to their projects. Seller verification, contracting and dispute handling will need explicit design.
3. **AI-assisted Building — vision:** assistant and builder workflows, not part of initial public release.

## Why IMOV might matter (hypotheses, not validated claims)

- Buyers waste time moving across scattered catalogs, freelancers and documentation.
- Creators need clearer ways to present what their code does and how it can be used.
- A unified discovery-to-build experience may reduce the friction between acquiring and adapting a product.

**Differentiation hypothesis:** clearer technical product information + better discovery-to-service handoff + an eventual AI-assisted workflow. This must be tested with user interviews, not presented as proven advantage.

## Phase-1 outcomes

- Potential user understands IMOV in about one screen of content.
- Primary CTA lands on `/explore` (no login wall).
- Potential collaborator understands the roadmap at `/vision`.
- The site renders credibly and quickly on mobile and desktop.
- We have a design system, maintainable Next.js foundation, and testable user journeys.

## Honesty rules

Do not invent: user counts, GMV, product counts, partner logos, testimonials, seller quality certification, escrow, secure-payment claims, on-platform payment support, a working AI product, or launch dates. Any UI data used to demonstrate future layouts must say **Concept preview** or **Demo content** and not mimic real inventory available to purchase.

## Scope boundaries

**In scope:** real marketing site, `/explore` information architecture and preview, vision page, metadata/SEO basics, responsive UX, static demos, reasonable animations, navigation tests.

**Out of scope for first vertical slice:** sign-in, buyer/seller accounts, live marketplace listings, payments, chat, purchase/download flows, Supabase integration, AI endpoints, waitlist data collection without confirmed consent and data management plan.

## Validation to do later

- 5–10 short conversations with buyers and sellers to understand what they need and what they trust.
- Evaluate actual search filters, product metadata, licensing expectations, and service discovery needs.
- Measure CTA clicks, meaningful exploration, and real expressions of interest once analytics/contact are correctly configured.
